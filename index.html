    About the Comparative Gospel Harmony

    The Comparative Gospel Harmony places the four Gospels — Matthew, Mark, Luke and John — side by side in the King James Version, arranging the life of Jesus Christ into 185 events in chronological order, so you can read every account of each event together and compare what each Gospel records. Listen to each event read aloud, walk the 30-day Life of Christ journey with daily discussion, test yourself with the Life of Christ Quiz, watch the Gospel story films, and bring your questions to Ask the Harmony — a Scripture chat that answers only from the Gospels themselves.
