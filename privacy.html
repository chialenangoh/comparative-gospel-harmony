Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Gospel Harmony (also called Comparative Gospel Harmony) is a free Bible reading app and website operated by Chialefack Claude Nangoh (Christvision Messages), Columbus, Ohio, USA. This policy explains, in plain words, what information the app handles and what it does not. If anything here is unclear, email us at chialenangoh@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We show no advertising and we never sell your information.
  • We use no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • Your reading progress, highlights and settings stay on your own device, unless you choose to create an account to sync them.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your reading position, listening progress, highlights, bookmarks and preferences (such as your chosen Bible version) in your device's local storage so the app can pick up where you left off and work offline. This information never leaves your device unless you sign in to sync it (see below). You can erase it at any time by clearing the app's or browser's site data.

Optional accounts and sync

Creating an account is optional. If you create one, our service provider Supabase stores your email address and your password (stored in securely hashed form — we cannot see your password), together with the reading progress, highlights and settings you choose to sync, so they are available on your other devices. We use this only to operate your account and sync your data. You may ask us to delete your account and synced data at any time by emailing chialenangoh@gmail.com.

Bible text requests

Most Bible text in the app is delivered with the app itself. When you choose the NKJV or NIV version, the passage text is fetched from the Scripture API service at api.scripture.api.bible. That request contains the book, chapter and passage you asked for; it does not contain your name, email address or account details.

What we do not collect

  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or files from your device.
  • No payment information (the app is free and sells nothing).
  • No advertising identifiers, and no data is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

Gospel Harmony is a general-audience Bible app suitable for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Accounts are optional for everyone, and the app works fully without one.

Security

Connections to our services use encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Account passwords are stored by our provider in hashed form. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to the minimum described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
